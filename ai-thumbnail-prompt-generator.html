What is the Thumbnail Prompt Generator?

A thumbnail prompt helps you create a stronger visual direction before generating images in tools like AI image generators. ShortsCraft's AI Thumbnail Prompt Generator writes English prompts that focus on subject, mood, lighting, composition, contrast, and YouTube-style readability. It is useful for creators who want professional-looking thumbnails without guessing how to describe the image. You still need to review the generated image, add text carefully, and make sure the final thumbnail matches your video topic.

ShortsCraft is not meant to replace your judgement. It gives you a useful draft, then you can improve the tone, remove anything inaccurate, and make the final output match your channel style. This combination of AI speed and human editing is the safest way to build a reliable content workflow.

How to use it

  1. Step 1: Enter your video topic or title.
  2. Step 2: Generate prompts using the Thumbnail tab.
  3. Step 3: Paste the prompt into your image tool and refine the output.
