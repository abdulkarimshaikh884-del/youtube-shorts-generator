What is the Title Generator?

A strong title can make your short feel more interesting before someone even starts watching. ShortsCraft's YouTube Shorts Title Generator helps creators turn a basic topic into multiple title ideas with curiosity, clarity, and a creator-friendly tone. It is made for fast content workflows where you need options quickly but still want to choose the best title manually. The goal is not to create clickbait; the goal is to help you package your idea in a way that viewers understand instantly.

ShortsCraft is not meant to replace your judgement. It gives you a useful draft, then you can improve the tone, remove anything inaccurate, and make the final output match your channel style. This combination of AI speed and human editing is the safest way to build a reliable content workflow.

How to use it

  1. Step 1: Write your video topic in simple words.
  2. Step 2: Generate title ideas using the Titles tab.
  3. Step 3: Pick the best option and adjust it to match your video style.

FAQ

Questions? Answered.

Should every Shorts video have a title?

Yes. Even if Shorts discovery depends heavily on the video itself, a clear title helps search, browsing, and channel presentation.

Will the title generator make clickbait?

It creates options, but you should choose honest titles that match your video content.

Can I use titles for Instagram Reels too?

Yes. Many title ideas can be adapted into captions, reel hooks, or on-screen text.

How many title options should I test?

Generate several options, then choose the one that is clear, short, and emotionally interesting.
