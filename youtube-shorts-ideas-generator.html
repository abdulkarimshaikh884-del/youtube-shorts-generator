What is the Ideas Generator?

Consistency becomes easier when you never run out of ideas. ShortsCraft's YouTube Shorts Ideas Generator helps creators expand one topic into multiple short-form video angles. This is useful when you want to build a content calendar, plan a series, or create related videos that keep viewers watching your channel. The ideas are meant to be starting points; you should choose the ones that match your audience, your niche, and your content style.

ShortsCraft is not meant to replace your judgement. It gives you a useful draft, then you can improve the tone, remove anything inaccurate, and make the final output match your channel style. This combination of AI speed and human editing is the safest way to build a reliable content workflow.

How to use it

  1. Step 1: Enter your niche, topic, or content category.
  2. Step 2: Generate ideas using the Ideas tab.
  3. Step 3: Pick the strongest ideas and build scripts or thumbnails for them.

Key benefits

No. Select only the ideas that match your audience and have clear value.

Yes. Most short-form ideas can be adapted for Reels, Shorts, or TikTok-style videos.
