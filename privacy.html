Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2025  ·  Effective immediately

01

Introduction

Welcome to ShortsCraft ("we", "us", or "our"). We operate the website shortscraft.online — a free AI-powered tool for generating YouTube Shorts scripts, titles, descriptions, and more.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information. By using ShortsCraft, you agree to the practices described here.

02

Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information depending on how you use ShortsCraft:

  • Account data — If you sign in (via Google OAuth through Supabase), we receive your name, email address, and profile picture from your Google account.
  • Generated content — Topics you enter, scripts, titles, descriptions, hashtags, and thumbnail prompts that are generated. These may be saved to your account if you choose.
  • Usage history — Timestamps and topics of content you generate, stored locally in your browser (localStorage) and optionally in your account.
  • Payment data — If you upgrade to Pro, your payment is processed by Razorpay. We do not store your card details. We only receive a payment confirmation and order ID.
  • Feedback submissions — Name, email, and messages submitted through our feedback form.
  • Technical data — Browser type, IP address, device type, and basic analytics (e.g., page views) collected automatically when you visit our site.
03

Cookies & Local Storage

ShortsCraft uses browser localStorage to store your script history and saved scripts locally on your device. This data does not leave your browser unless you are signed in.

We may also use cookies for:

  • Maintaining your login session (via Supabase Auth)
  • Basic analytics and performance monitoring (e.g., Google Analytics, if enabled)
  • Remembering your preferences (tab state, UI settings)

You can clear localStorage and cookies at any time through your browser settings.

04

Third-Party Services

We rely on the following third-party services to power ShortsCraft. Each has their own privacy policy:

  • Groq API (LLaMA model) — Processes the topics you submit to generate AI content. Your input is sent to Groq's servers.
  • Supabase — Handles user authentication, account storage, saved scripts, and usage credits.
  • Razorpay — Processes Pro plan payments securely. We never see your full card details.
  • Google OAuth — Used for Sign In with Google functionality.
  • Google Analytics — May be used to understand site usage and improve the product.
  • Render — Our hosting platform where the ShortsCraft server runs.
05

How We Use Your Data

We use collected data to:

  • Provide and improve ShortsCraft's features
  • Manage your account, saved scripts, and usage credits
  • Process Pro plan subscriptions and verify payments
  • Respond to your feedback and support requests
  • Monitor site performance and fix bugs
  • Improve AI prompt quality based on anonymized usage patterns

🔒 We do not sell your personal data to any third party. Ever.

06

Data Retention

We retain your account data as long as your account is active. Script history stored in localStorage is controlled entirely by you and deleted when you clear your browser data.

You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by emailing us.
