🎯 Our mission

Indian creators write, record, and edit alone. Coming up with hooks, titles, descriptions, hashtags, and thumbnails for every upload eats hours every day. ShortsCraft was built to give every creator — whether you have 100 subs or 1M — access to professional-grade content drafts in seconds, completely free.

Great content should be about creativity — not formatting and copy-pasting. Let AI handle the first draft. You add the personality, the experience, and the local flavour.

👥 Who is it for?

  • Beginner creators
  • Daily-posting channels
  • Faceless / cashflow channels
  • Educators & coaches
  • Local-language creators

⚙️ How it works

Powered by Groq (LLaMA models), tuned with Hinglish-aware prompts. Each generation produces a structured output your YouTube workflow can use immediately.

🛡️ Review-first & AdSense-safe
