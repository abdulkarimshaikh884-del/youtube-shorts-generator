Plan questions

Why show monthly credits if they refresh daily?

The monthly number makes plans easy to compare. The daily refresh protects the export queue and gives you a predictable new balance every day.

Can every paid subscriber use every AI option?

Yes. Pro and Pro Max unlock Standard, Detailed and Advanced generation; each option uses 2, 5 or 8 credits.

What happens when a render fails?

If generation or export fails on our side after charging, that operation's credits are returned automatically.

Does the yearly plan include verification?

Yes. The verified creator badge stays active for the paid yearly subscription period. ShortsCraft's own @shortscraft account is permanently verified.

Are Stars the same as credits?

No. Credits pay for generation and export. Stars are non-cash appreciation that creators can give to one another.
