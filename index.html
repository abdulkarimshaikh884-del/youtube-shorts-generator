FAQ

Questions? Answered.

Is ShortsCraft free?

Yes. ShortsCraft has a free plan so creators can generate basic content without a card.

Can I use generated scripts commercially?

You can use outputs for your videos, but always review, edit, and fact-check before publishing.

Does AI guarantee viral videos?

No. ShortsCraft helps with drafts and ideas, but views depend on topic, packaging, editing, retention, and audience response.

Should I edit AI output before publishing?

Yes. Treat ShortsCraft output as a strong first draft. Always review facts, improve voice, and adjust it for your audience before publishing.

Is this tool for beginners?

Yes. It is designed for beginners, faceless channels, and creators who want a faster workflow.

Does it support YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels?

Yes. The ideas, hooks, hashtags, and scripts can be used for YouTube Shorts and adapted for Instagram Reels.

Built responsibly for creators

ShortsCraft generates AI-assisted drafts. Users should review, edit, and fact-check all output before publishing. ShortsCraft does not guarantee views, earnings, or virality.
