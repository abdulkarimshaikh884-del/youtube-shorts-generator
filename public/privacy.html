🔐 Privacy & data

Privacy Policy

This page explains what information ShortsCraft may collect, how it is used, and how you can manage your data.

Last updated: 2026 ShortsCraft Creator workspace
01

What ShortsCraft does

ShortsCraft is an AI-assisted creator workspace that helps users generate animated YouTube Shorts, motion graphics previews, kinetic typography, scripts, titles, descriptions, hashtags, ideas, and thumbnail prompts.

02

Information we collect

When you use the generator, we may receive the topic, script, prompt, selected style, or form inputs you submit so the tool can return the requested output. Contact forms may collect your name, email address, subject, and message. Some workspace data such as saved scripts, history, and preferences may be stored locally in your browser.

03

How we use information

We use submitted information to generate content, improve the product, provide support, prevent abuse, and maintain free-tier limits. We do not sell your personal information.

04

AI providers and third-party services

If AI generation is connected to an external model provider, your submitted topic or script may be sent to that provider only for processing your request. Payment, analytics, or hosting providers may process limited technical data when those features are enabled.

05

Cookies and analytics

ShortsCraft may use basic analytics to understand traffic and product usage. We aim to keep analytics privacy-conscious and focused on aggregate product improvement.

06

Data security

We use HTTPS on production deployments and reasonable security practices. No online service can guarantee absolute security, so avoid submitting highly sensitive personal information into AI fields.

07

Your choices

You can clear local browser data, avoid submitting personal information in prompts, and contact us through the contact page for privacy-related questions.

08

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any important changes will be reflected on this page.
