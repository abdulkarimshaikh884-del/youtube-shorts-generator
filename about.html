About ShortsCraft

A free AI workspace for YouTube Shorts creators

ShortsCraft helps creators turn simple video topics into practical short-form content ideas, scripts, titles, descriptions, hashtags, and thumbnail prompts. It is built to make the planning stage faster, cleaner, and easier for anyone creating YouTube Shorts.

What ShortsCraft does

ShortsCraft is a free AI tool for creators who want quick inspiration for YouTube Shorts. You can type a topic and generate a content package that includes a script, title suggestions, description copy, hashtags, new ideas, and thumbnail prompts.

The goal is simple: help creators move from idea to draft faster while keeping the editing and final creative control in their own hands.

Who it is for

ShortsCraft is useful for YouTubers, students, educators, editors, influencers, small business owners, and faceless channel creators who publish short-form videos regularly.

Whether you make facts, motivation, tutorials, entertainment, or educational Shorts, the tool can help you create a clear starting point.

How creators can use it

  • Scripts: Generate short-form video scripts that you can adjust for your voiceover style.
  • Titles and descriptions: Create useful metadata ideas for YouTube uploads.
  • Hashtags: Get relevant hashtag suggestions for discoverability.
  • Ideas: Find new angles when you are stuck or planning multiple uploads.
  • Thumbnail prompts: Create prompt ideas for AI image tools or thumbnail design inspiration.

Important content note

ShortsCraft is designed to support creativity, not replace human review. AI-generated content may need corrections, fact-checking, tone changes, or additional context before publishing. Creators should always review the output, remove anything inaccurate, and make the final content original and suitable for their audience.
