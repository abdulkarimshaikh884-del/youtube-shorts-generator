What is the Pricing?

ShortsCraft is designed to help creators start without pressure. The free plan gives beginners a simple way to test the AI generator, create scripts, titles, hashtags, descriptions, ideas, and thumbnail prompts. When creators need more daily usage, saved workflow features, or a faster production routine, the Pro plan can support a more serious content schedule. Pricing should stay simple and transparent so users understand what they get before upgrading.

ShortsCraft is not meant to replace your judgement. It gives you a useful draft, then you can improve the tone, remove anything inaccurate, and make the final output match your channel style. This combination of AI speed and human editing is the safest way to build a reliable content workflow.

How to use it

  2. Step 2: Create your first scripts, titles, and prompts.
  3. Step 3: Upgrade to Pro when you need more usage and a stronger workflow.
