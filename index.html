⚡ About ShortsCraft

What is ShortsCraft?

ShortsCraft is a free AI-powered content tool built specifically for YouTube Shorts creators in India. Whether you are just starting your channel or already posting consistently, ShortsCraft helps you skip the hardest part of content creation — the blank page.

Just type your video topic and ShortsCraft instantly generates a complete Hinglish script (with Hook, Main Content, and CTA), five SEO-optimized video titles, a ready-to-paste YouTube description, fifteen relevant hashtags, seven fresh content ideas, and three AI thumbnail prompts for tools like Midjourney and DALL-E — all in one click.

The tool is designed for Indian creators who speak and create in Hinglish — the natural mix of Hindi and English that resonates with audiences across India. No technical knowledge is needed. No credit card required. The free plan gives you five generations per day, and Pro users get unlimited access for just ₹99/month.

ShortsCraft uses advanced large language models to produce content that feels conversational, energetic, and culturally relevant — not robotic. That said, we always recommend reviewing AI-generated content before publishing to ensure it matches your voice and meets YouTube's community guidelines.

Our goal is simple: give every creator in India the tools to compete, grow, and build an audience — without spending hours on scripting and metadata every single day.
