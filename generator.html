Why is the generator on a separate page?

It creates a cleaner, more premium homepage while giving the tools a dedicated workspace of their own.

Can I save scripts?

Yes. Sign in and you can save scripts to your account and revisit them later.

Should I review AI output?

Absolutely. Always edit, review and fact-check before publishing.

Does ShortsCraft support only YouTube?

The outputs are optimized for YouTube Shorts, but many ideas and scripts can also be adapted for Reels and other short-form platforms.

Use AI as a smart assistant, not autopilot

ShortsCraft creates fast, structured drafts to help creators move quicker. Final quality still depends on your review, packaging, edit style and audience understanding.
