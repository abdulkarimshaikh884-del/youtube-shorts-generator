⚙️ How It Works

ShortsCraft uses large language models (LLaMA via Groq API) trained on massive amounts of text to understand your topic and generate contextually relevant, creative content in Hinglish — the natural mix of Hindi and English spoken by millions of Indians every day.

Here's the process:

  • You enter a topic — anything from "morning routine" to "stock market basics"
  • Our AI generates a full script, 5 titles, an SEO description, 15 hashtags, 7 content ideas, and 3 thumbnail prompts simultaneously
  • All output appears in organized tabs so you can copy exactly what you need
  • Signed-in users can save scripts to their account for later reference

The entire generation process typically takes under 10 seconds. No waiting, no queues.

⚠️ Important: Review Before You Publish

ShortsCraft generates content using artificial intelligence. While the output is designed to be high-quality and useful, AI-generated content should always be reviewed by a human before publishing.

  • Verify any factual claims made in the generated script
  • Edit the content to match your personal voice and style
  • Ensure the content complies with YouTube's Community Guidelines
  • Do not republish AI content without reading and understanding it first

Think of ShortsCraft as a smart first draft — you bring the creativity, authenticity, and final judgment. The AI just saves you the blank-page problem.
